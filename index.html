Make a ringtone from audio you already have.

Pick a song file, a video or a voice memo, choose the best 29 seconds and set it as your ringtone. Your first ringtone is free.

Coming soon to the App Store iPhone · iOS 18 or later

How it works

  1. Pick the sound. A file from Files or iCloud Drive, a video or screen recording, a voice memo, a song stored on your iPhone, or a new recording.
  2. Choose the part. Drag the handles, play it back and add a fade in or out. A ringtone can be up to 29.5 seconds long.
  3. Set it. On iOS 26 and later, tap Share Ringtone, then Use as Ringtone. On iOS 18 to 25, Plainring walks you through GarageBand, Apple's free app.

Songs from Apple Music and other copy-protected audio can't be used: iOS doesn't let any app read them.

One price, no subscription

After your free first ringtone, Plainring Unlimited is a one-time purchase: $4.99 in the US, and the App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy. It never renews. There are no ads, no account and no trial to cancel.

Your audio stays on your iPhone

Plainring has no servers and collects no data. Everything happens on your iPhone, and it works offline. Read the privacy policy.
